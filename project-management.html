Division 03

Project
Management

End-to-end delivery management ensuring your real estate projects are completed on time, on budget, and to the highest possible standard.

Our Approach

Delivering projects
with precision

Successful real estate development requires meticulous coordination across a wide range of disciplines — architects, engineers, contractors, consultants and funders. TMP's project management team provides the oversight, accountability and commercial rigour needed to bring complex projects to completion.



We act as the client's representative throughout the development process, managing cost, programme and quality while maintaining strong relationships with all parties in the delivery chain.

What We Do

Our project management
capabilities

01

Programme Management

Detailed programme planning and monitoring from pre-construction through to practical completion — identifying risks early and keeping delivery on track.

02

Cost Management

Rigorous cost planning, procurement oversight and change control to ensure projects are delivered within budget without compromising quality or specification.

03

Stakeholder Management

Co-ordinating across design teams, contractors, funders, local authorities and end users — maintaining clear communication and aligned objectives throughout.

04

Procurement & Tendering

Preparing procurement strategies, managing tender processes and appointing contractors and consultants on the most advantageous terms for our clients.

05

Risk Management

Proactive identification and mitigation of project risks — from ground conditions and planning obligations through to contractor insolvency and market changes.

06

Handover & Completion

Managing the critical path to practical completion, snagging, handover and occupation — ensuring a smooth transition from construction to operational asset.
