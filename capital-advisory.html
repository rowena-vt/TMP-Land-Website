Capital
Advisory

Debt and equity placement, development finance and structured investment solutions — connecting capital with opportunity across all real estate sectors.

Our Approach

Connecting capital
with opportunity

TMP's Capital Advisory team provides independent, relationship-driven advice to developers, investors and landowners seeking to raise debt or equity for their real estate projects. We leverage deep lender and investor relationships to deliver the right capital solution — efficiently and on the best available terms.



Our independence means we work solely in our clients' interests, presenting opportunities to the widest appropriate universe of capital providers and negotiating hard on their behalf.

Service 01

Debt Placement

We place senior debt, mezzanine finance and whole loan facilities for income-producing and transitional real estate assets. Our lender panel covers clearing banks, challenger banks, debt funds and insurance company lenders — ensuring competitive terms and appropriate capital structures.


  • → Senior investment debt
  • → Mezzanine and stretched senior facilities
  • → Whole loan and pari passu structures
  • → Refinancing and restructuring

Service 02

Equity Placement

Raising equity for real estate projects requires a different skill set to debt — understanding investor return requirements, governance preferences and sector appetite. TMP connects developers and asset managers with institutional equity, family office capital and private equity real estate funds.


  • → Institutional equity and club deals
  • → Family office and HNWI equity
  • → Private equity real estate funds
  • → Co-investment and co-GP structures

Service 03

Development Finance

Development finance is one of the most complex and relationship-dependent areas of real estate capital. TMP advises developers on the full capital stack — from land acquisition finance through to construction loan and eventual investment refinancing — ensuring the right structure at every stage.


  • → Land acquisition and bridging finance
  • → Senior development loans
  • → Mezzanine development finance
  • → Forward funding and pre-sale structures

Investment Structuring

The right investment structure can materially affect returns, risk allocation and execution certainty. TMP advises on the optimal vehicle and structure for real estate investments — working alongside legal and tax advisors to ensure the chosen approach is fit for purpose.


  • → SPV and holding company structures
  • → REIT and fund structures
  • → Share vs asset sale considerations
  • → Cross-border investment structuring

JV & Partnerships

Joint ventures and strategic partnerships unlock opportunities that neither party could access alone.
