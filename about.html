Our Story

At TMP Land
Consultants

We specialise in land, development, and off-market property opportunities across the UK. Working closely with landowners, developers, investors, and local authorities, we provide strategic advice across acquisitions, disposals, development land, freehold blocks, portfolios, and commercial investments.


With a growing network of active buyers and investors, we focus on sourcing and structuring opportunities that align with our clients' objectives — from residential redevelopment and strategic land promotion to income-producing investments and value-add opportunities.


Our team combines market knowledge, commercial awareness, and a proactive approach to deal-making, helping clients navigate transactions efficiently and confidentially. Whether acting on acquisitions, disposals, or off-market instructions, we pride ourselves on building long-term relationships and delivering opportunities with clarity, integrity, and professionalism.

The Team

Meet the people
behind TMP

Managing Director

Bradley Thompson

Bradley Thompson is the Managing Director of TMP Land Consultants, leading the firm's growth across land, development, and off-market investment opportunities throughout the UK.


With a strong focus on acquisitions, disposals, and strategic land opportunities, Bradley works closely with landowners, developers, investors, and funding partners to structure and source opportunities across residential, commercial, and mixed-use sectors. He is actively involved in building long-term client relationships and identifying opportunities with strong development or investment potential.

Head of Capital Markets

Fin McCormick

Fin McCormick oversees capital markets activity at TMP Land Consultants, supporting investors, developers, and clients across acquisitions, investment opportunities, and redevelopment projects.


With experience across residential and commercial property, Fin plays a key role in connecting buyers with off-market opportunities and advising on transactions with asset management and development potential. His collaborative and proactive approach supports clients throughout the lifecycle of a deal, from initial sourcing through to completion.

Head of Agency

Joe Rea

Joe Rea leads agency operations at TMP Land Consultants, working across acquisitions, disposals, and off-market property instructions throughout the UK.


With a commercially focused approach, Joe works closely with investors, developers, and landowners to deliver opportunities across freehold blocks, portfolios, development land, and commercial investments. He plays an integral role in maintaining strong client relationships while supporting the firm's expanding national network of buyers and property opportunities.
