Our Story

At TMP Land
Consultants

We specialise in land, development, and off-market property opportunities across the UK. Working closely with landowners, developers, investors, and local authorities, we provide strategic advice across acquisitions, disposals, development land, freehold blocks, portfolios, and commercial investments.


With a growing network of active buyers and investors, we focus on sourcing and structuring opportunities that align with our clients' objectives — from residential redevelopment and strategic land promotion to income-producing investments and value-add opportunities.


Our team combines market knowledge, commercial awareness, and a proactive approach to deal-making, helping clients navigate transactions efficiently and confidentially. Whether acting on acquisitions, disposals, or off-market instructions, we pride ourselves on building long-term relationships and delivering opportunities with clarity, integrity, and professionalism.

The Team

Meet the people
behind TMP

Co Founder & Managing Director

Bradley Thompson

Bradley Thompson is Co Founder and Managing Director of TMP Land Consultants, leading the firm's strategy and growth across the UK's most complex and high-value real estate transactions.


Bradley specialises in trophy assets and large-scale portfolio transactions, bringing a deep network of relationships with institutional investors, pension funds, and sovereign wealth capital. His commercial instinct and ability to access off-market mandates at the highest level sets TMP apart, enabling clients to transact with discretion and conviction across all major asset classes.

Co Founder & Senior Partner

Fin McCormick

Fin McCormick is Co Founder and Senior Partner at TMP Land Consultants, overseeing project management across the firm's most active mandates and sector verticals.


Fin leads delivery across capital markets, renewables, residential development, and mixed-use projects, ensuring transactions are structured and executed with precision from instruction through to completion. His cross-sector expertise and hands-on project management approach allows TMP to manage complex, multi-party deals with clarity and pace.

Origination Executive

Joe Rea

Joe Rea is an Origination Executive at TMP Land Consultants, focused on proactively sourcing deal flow and generating off-market opportunities across the UK.


Joe combines rigorous market analytics with on-the-ground intelligence to identify and qualify opportunities before they reach the open market. From conducting detailed site and portfolio analysis to building relationships with landowners, developers, and agents, Joe plays a central role in keeping TMP at the forefront of the most active deal pipeline in the market.
