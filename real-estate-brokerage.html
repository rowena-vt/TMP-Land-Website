Consented Developments

Sites and assets with planning consent represent a distinct investment opportunity. TMP advises on the marketing and disposal of consented development opportunities, understanding how to present and position schemes to achieve the strongest possible outcomes.

  • Commercial and hotel development land
  • Phased delivery strategies
  • Deferred payment and overage structures

Strategic Land

Strategic land — sites without current planning consent but with development potential — requires long-term thinking and patient capital. TMP advises landowners, promoters and developers on the identification, promotion and disposal of strategic land opportunities across the UK.

  • Greenfield and brownfield strategic land
  • Promotion agreements and options
  • Allocation and local plan engagement
  • Land assembly and agricultural land
